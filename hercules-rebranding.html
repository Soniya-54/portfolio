Overview

Hercules Touch Anesthesia helps dental anesthesia teams document cases, manage compliance, and review operational data. At DevDash Labs, I worked on a refreshed identity and website to make that value easier to understand.

The challenge

HTA serves a specialized audience and supports important clinical workflows. The brand and website needed to explain the product clearly while earning trust in a healthcare setting. The experience also had to speak to practitioners who work across locations and rely on mobile tools.

What I focused on

Create a cohesive identity

I brought color, typography, and visual elements together to make the brand feel modern, professional, and dependable.

Make the product easier to understand

The website explains documentation, compliance, and analytics in plain language, supported by clear product visuals.

Design for the people using it

Responsive layouts, clear demo and product links, lead forms, and an AI chatbot help busy practitioners explore the platform and get in touch.

The result

The refreshed identity and website give Hercules a clearer way to introduce the platform, show how it works, and invite practices to request a demo. The site brings the brand and product story together for its specialized audience.
