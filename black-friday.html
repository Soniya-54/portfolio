A Black Friday offer people can understand

A campaign page that puts the offer, the savings, and the final price in one clear view.

Role

Creative Designer

Scope

Tools

Visit the live page ↗

Overview

This campaign offered one year of Programiz PRO and a second year free, with 40% off the first year. I designed a landing page to make the offer easy to scan and the total cost easy to find.

The challenge

The deal includes several numbers: the discount, the free year, and the amount due. If those details compete for attention, it can be hard to understand what the offer actually costs.

What I focused on

Lead with the offer

The headline explains the two-year deal before introducing supporting details.

Show the math beside the message

A simple breakdown explains the first-year price, the free second year, and the total due.

Keep the next step easy to find

The offer and call to action sit together, with the campaign deadline nearby.

The result

The page brings the offer, savings breakdown, deadline, and call to action together. Visitors can understand the deal before deciding whether to claim it.
