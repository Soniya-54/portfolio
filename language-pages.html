Overview

People looking for resources in a specific language or topic had to browse and filter a broad course catalog. I worked on dedicated pages for Python, SQL, HTML/CSS, JavaScript/React, DSA, C, C++, and Java so learners could start from the subject they came to learn.

The challenge

The catalog gave learners a way to find courses, but it did not offer a focused page for each language or topic. We also wanted people to discover learning paths first. A path gives them a way to keep building skills over the months they are subscribed, with individual courses easy to access along the way.

What I focused on

Create a page for each subject

Each page gives learners looking for a particular language or topic a direct place to start.

Put learning paths first

The pages introduce a longer learning journey before listing individual courses, making the subscription's ongoing value easier to see.

Keep courses easy to reach

Learners can still get to the specific courses and resources they came for without having to work through catalog filters.

What we saw

About a week after publishing, we reviewed around 15 to 20 Microsoft Clarity sessions. In those sessions, we saw users clicking into learning paths and getting to courses more easily.
